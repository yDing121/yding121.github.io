About a week ago, a very good friend of mine came to me distressed, asking for help and insights regarding the future of her career. As a bio student at a very reputable university, she had gotten herself a summer job at a research lab. Upon arriving at the lab on her first day, she discovered that the main responsibility of her position was to process data. Asking around her coworkers, she found that much of their work involved extracting insights from raw experimental data utilizing machine learning techniques. This all looked very scary to a bio student whose only prior exposure to data analysis was introductory statistics.
